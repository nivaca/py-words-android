<p>py-words is a Latin-English-Latin and Latin-Spanish-Latin dictionary with inflectional morphology: give it a Latin form, and it says what the form can be (case, number, tense, person …) and what the word means; give it an English or Spanish word, and it finds the Latin ones. It is based on William Whitaker's WORDS.</p>

<p>This app shows py-words from one of two sources, and a menu at the top changes from one to the other:</p>

<ul>
<li><b>On this phone</b>: py-words itself runs inside the app, without the Internet. The app carries the program, its data and a Python to run it. This is the default.</li>
<li><b>Online</b>: the site py-words.uniandes.edu.co.</li>
</ul>

<p>Both show the same page, with the same searches, results and settings.</p>

<ul>
<li>From any app: select a Latin word or line and choose <i>Look up in py-words</i>, or share a text with the app.</li>
<li>Back returns to the previous page; links to anything else open in the browser.</li>
<li>Light and dark mode, as the system says; English and Spanish interface.</li>
<li>Only Logeion, an online source that py-words can also search, needs the Internet on the phone.</li>
</ul>

<p>It needs a 64-bit ARM device (arm64), as nearly all Android phones and tablets are.</p>
