• Each release also carries SHA256SUMS, with the SHA-256 of the APK, and its OpenPGP signature SHA256SUMS.asc; 2.5 got them afterwards. Before tagging or pushing, tools/update-py-words.py checks every signature with only the public key, as a user would, and the signature of the tag, and stops if one fails.
• tools/update-py-words.py also releases changes of the app alone, with the same py-words, when the changelogs have an "Unreleased" section (2.5 was the first).
